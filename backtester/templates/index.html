LUNE / TICK BACKTESTERINDIAN STANDARD TIME

Pivots per index

R
The unit stops, targets, locks and filters are measured in, as a multiple of the index's own all-in round-trip cost.
Threshold
How far price must turn from its extreme before a pivot prints, × cost. 0: R does. With min leg 0 it is the leg itself: every leg is at least this long, each pivot printed on the first tick that far back. Bigger: bigger legs, later prints.
Min leg
0 (big legs): off, nothing is ever forced. Above 0 (Lune live): no pivot prints before the swing from the last pivot is this long, × cost, so the threshold can be smaller and prints sooner; but a pivot price then breaks forces the next one on a short leg.
Points
At the latest session's open.
